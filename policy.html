privacy policy

grace re · last updated: 2026-09-08

what this covers

this policy explains what data the game grace re collects and how it is used. it applies to the game itself, not to this website or any other service you may use.

data we collect

to play online you sign in with an epic games account through epic online services (eos). using that account we receive:

  • your epic account id and display name
  • your friends list and presence, which is used to let you join friends in the gathering lobby
  • voice and short presence updates during a lobby session (only while you are in a shared lobby)
  • game save data — your progress and in-game currency — which is kept on your device and mirrored to the eos cloud so it follows you between devices

advertising and tracking

there is no advertising, no telemetry, and no tracking. we do not run analytics or share your data with anybody except epic games where the online service itself requires it.

who processes the data

the game has no servers of its own. when you play online, data such as your account id and voice packets are handled by epic games through the eos service, under epic's own terms and privacy policy. we never see or store your data on our own infrastructure because none exists.

everything else lives on your device: the local save file in godot's user:// directory is encrypted on disk and only transmitted to the eos cloud to sync it between your own devices.

retention and deletion

  • local save — removed if you uninstall the game or delete the folder godot/app_userdata/grace re
  • cloud save — deleted when you request it (see contact below); syncing pauses while you are signed out
  • epic account data — managed by epic's privacy policy

minors

online play requires an epic account, which enforces its own minimum age. we do not knowingly collect data from children below the age your local law requires for online services.

changes

if this policy changes, the date above is updated. continued use of the game after a change means you accept the new policy.
