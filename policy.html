grace re — privacy policy

last updated september 2026

grace re uses epic online services (eos) to provide online features. this policy describes what data is accessed and stored.

data collected through eos

  • epic games display name — shown above your character in multiplayer lobbies.
  • epic games friends list — used only for the in-game friends display; not stored or shared.
  • cloud-saved progression — bricks, level, and xp stored in your epic player data storage so your save follows you across devices.
  • real-time position data — your in-world position is broadcast to other players in the shared lobby so everyone can see each other move.

none of the above is collected, sold, or shared outside of what eos provides.

epic online services

all online features are powered by epic online services. for how epic handles data, see the epic games privacy policy.
